My entry to the make.fun bounty [news-image-20261123](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123):
one image that sums up the world news of Monday 23 November 2026.

The image is a one-page newspaper front page, drawn as an HTML page (hand-written markup and inline
SVG icons) and rendered to a 1024×1024 PNG of about 260 KB:

- **Masthead and date**, named in full: *Monday, 23 November 2026*, with a world-clock strip
  (Tokyo, Jakarta, Dubai, Brussels, London, New York) showing the day closing across time zones.
- **Lead story**: Huang returns as Nvidia CEO — reinstated after 11 days, shares soar 31% and the
  Nasdaq jumps 6.5%.
- **36 more stories across nine regions**, each with its own small illustrated card:
  Asia markets slide and Seoul readies a rescue fund; the Dymer toll reaches 11; the Cikande toll
  reaches at least 24; Belgium's national strike grounds its flights; Shuri Castle rises again;
  the Pentagon rebukes Canada; Burnham sells his EU reset; the EU proposes its 22nd sanctions
  package; FIFA names the 2031 and 2035 Women's World Cup hosts; NATO's Rutte warns Russia's war
  machine won't stop; Ukraine hits 16 missile launchers; drones hit a Russian refinery; Zelensky
  confirms a Russian warship sunk off Libya; Bolsonaro is diagnosed with pneumonia; the COP31
  blame game erupts in Australia; India gives Nepal a $1.62bn package; China sentences an official
  to death for spying; the US blacklists a Singapore chip firm; Hong Kong orders fire reform; the
  US sanctions Iran's shadow banking network; Iran vows to break the blockade; Steinmeier starts an
  Africa tour; CrowdStrike buys Zscaler for $43.9bn; HPE beats earnings and raises its outlook;
  NASA briefs the Cygnus cargo flight; Brentford thrash Everton 4–0; the Commanders stun the
  Bengals 22–20; the GTA VI album goes deluxe; Spielberg is to direct his first Western; Merz calls
  NATO's 2008 refusal on Ukraine a mistake; an Israeli strike kills an UNRWA worker in Gaza;
  Erdogan defends the failed COP31 summit; a ground stop hits Chicago O'Hare; the UN chief urges
  global AI rules; India rises to third in the Asia Power Index; Mexico will try "El Chapo
  Isidro" at home.
- **A regions strip** naming all nine regions covered (North America, Latin America, Europe,
  Ukraine, Middle East, Asia-Pacific, South Asia, Africa, Oceania) and a footer noting where tolls
  on breaking stories were still moving.

Every story was checked against its source article's own text to confirm it happened, or was first
reported, on Monday 23 November 2026; where a toll was still moving it is phrased as a toll update
or with "at least".
